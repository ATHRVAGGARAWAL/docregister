# Third-party notices

## University of Dundee permanent-tooth models

The production anatomical dental-arch asset pipeline is configured for models published by the [University of Dundee, School of Dentistry](https://sketchfab.com/DundeeDental) in its [Permanent Teeth collection](https://sketchfab.com/DundeeDental/collections/permanent-teeth-4c0d0548c40c463c8cdceb6e0d08df7f).

License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Generated derivatives will be simplified, mirrored where necessary, re-oriented, scaled and assembled for interactive use. The exact source-model IDs and tooth mapping are recorded in `public/models/dental/attribution.json`. The Carabelli-variant model is intentionally excluded.

The current application renders a procedural low-detail arch until an authorized Sketchfab download is run. No Sketchfab asset is fetched by the browser.
